# STAR — AkıllıKobi: SaaS Platform for SMEs

> **Company:** Personal Venture
> **Period:** Early career project (pre-2020)
> **Role:** Sole Developer
> **Evidence:** 1,700 commits in `akillikobi` repository — the largest single-repo project by commit count

---

## Situation

Small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) in Turkey needed an affordable, all-in-one business management platform. Existing solutions were either too expensive (enterprise ERPs like SAP) or too simplistic (basic invoicing tools). Turkish SMEs needed a platform that handled their specific requirements: Turkish tax regulations, e-invoice integration, inventory management, and customer relationship tracking — all in Turkish.

## Task

I set out to build a **comprehensive SaaS application** for Turkish SMEs from scratch, covering:

- Business management (CRM, invoicing, inventory)
- Turkish-specific requirements (tax compliance, e-invoice format)
- Multi-tenant architecture for SaaS delivery
- User-friendly interface for non-technical business owners

## Action

### Full-Stack Development
- Built the entire application as a **solo developer** over an extended development period
- Committed **1,700 times** — my most prolific single project by commit count
- Implemented full-stack features: backend API, database design, frontend UI, deployment
- Designed for Turkish SME workflows with localized business logic

### SaaS Architecture
- Implemented multi-tenant data isolation for SaaS delivery
- Built user management, subscription handling, and role-based access control
- Created modular feature sets that could be enabled/disabled per tenant plan

### Business Domain Expertise
- Developed deep understanding of SME business operations
- Implemented Turkish tax calculation and e-invoice formatting
- Built inventory management with stock tracking and low-stock alerts
- Created CRM features for customer relationship management

## Result

- **1,700 commits** — demonstrating sustained development effort and iterative improvement
- Complete SaaS platform built as a solo developer
- Gained deep expertise in multi-tenant architecture that directly informed later DropXML design
- Developed strong full-stack development skills used throughout subsequent career
- The project served as a proving ground for architectural patterns (multi-tenancy, SaaS, event-driven) later refined in professional roles
